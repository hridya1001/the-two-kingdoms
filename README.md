# The Two Kingdoms -- An Atlas of Paths
A fun, creative and interactive web-based mini-game to visualize Breadth-First Search and Depth-First Search algorithms of graph traversal through the illustration of two kingdoms :)


### Kingdom I -- The Lost Messenger (BFS)
A messenger must reach the king's tower by the shortest possible route. The player blocks or clears roads on the shown map, then sends the messenger. BFS is shown exactly as it works: every road on the way is tested simultaneously (pulsing blue), ring by ring until the tower is reached. The shortest path is then traced in gold, road by road, as the messenger physically travels it. If all the roads are fully blocked, it says so too!

### Kingdom II -- The Treasure Maze (DFS)
An explorer searches a cave for treasure, always pressing forward down an unexplored tunnel until he hits a dead end. He then backtracks. The player seals or clears tunnel walls before pressing go. DFS is then shown naturally: footprints (amber) mark every tunnel walked, dead ends turn gray once fully explored, and backtracking is visibly animated in reverse along a dashed retreat trail. The chest glows gold if found.
