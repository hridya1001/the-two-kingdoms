Kingdom II

The Treasure Maze

Somewhere in these tunnels waits a chest of old gold. The explorer never turns back while a tunnel still lies ahead of him. Only when rock closes in on every side does he retrace his steps. Build the walls, then light his torch.

Click any tunnel to seal it with stone, or clear it again. Then light the torch and watch him walk until he finds gold or exhausts every tunnel.
